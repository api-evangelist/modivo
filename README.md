MODIVO is a Polish multibrand fashion and lifestyle retailer that operates one of the largest fashion e-commerce platforms in Central and Eastern Europe, selling clothing, footwear, accessories, beauty and home products from more than a thousand brands across Poland, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Romania, Hungary, Ukraine, the Baltics and Western Europe. MODIVO S.A. is the listed parent of the former CCC Group (renamed MODIVO S.A. in February 2026) and the group behind the eobuwie.pl, CCC, HalfPrice, worldbox and DeeZee retail brands; the MODIVO storefront company itself is the former eobuwie.pl S.A., a consumer-technology investment of SoftBank Vision Fund. MODIVO does not run a developer portal, but its storefront is an Adobe Commerce (Magento 2.4) deployment that serves two live, publicly readable machine contracts from its own domain: a self-describing Swagger 2.0 REST schema at https://modivo.pl/rest/all/schema?services=all and an openly introspectable GraphQL endpoint at https://modivo.pl/graphql. Third-party sellers integrate through a Mirakl-hosted marketplace tenant, and brands buy sponsored placements through MODIVO Ads, the group's retail-media platform spanning MODIVO and eobuwie.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for MODIVO: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
